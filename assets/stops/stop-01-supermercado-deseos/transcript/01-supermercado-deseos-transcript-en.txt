Welcome to Corralejo—and to the very first stop on your Discover Corralejo adventure: Deseos, the supermarket with a rather unusual origin story.

This place was once Inpescasa, a legendary local supermarket. When its owners suddenly decided to close, two entrepreneurs working nearby were faced with a very serious problem: they came here almost every day for their beers… so where were they supposed to get their cervezas now?

Unwilling to accept such a tragic fate, they moved heaven and earth to reopen the shop—and named it Deseos, meaning “wishes.” Apparently, some wishes really do come true… especially when cold beer is involved.

Today, Deseos is far more than a supermarket. Sitting close to the harbour, it is a favourite first stop for visitors arriving from Lanzarote, local boat crews, marina workers, nearby offices and Corralejo residents.

Inside, you’ll find an impressive variety of cocktails and mocktails, frozen treats, cold drinks, ready-to-eat food, beach essentials and practically everything you might need—whether you are here for a week’s holiday or lucky enough to call Fuerteventura home.

You can also leave your luggage here while you explore Corralejo or rent a bicycle and discover the town on two wheels.
So take a look around, grab something refreshing and get ready—your journey through Corralejo starts right here, with a wish.
