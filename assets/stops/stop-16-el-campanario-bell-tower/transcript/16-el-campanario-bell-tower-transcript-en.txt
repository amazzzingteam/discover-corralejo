You are at El Campanario in Corralejo. Step into this small Canarian-style courtyard, where white walls, wooden balconies, and the central bell tower set the rhythm of the place. If you listen carefully, you can hear the gentle sound of the fountain and the music drifting from the bars around the square.

Look around you. This is not a typical shopping center, but a peaceful open square designed to resemble a traditional Canarian village. On certain days, especially during the craft market, the space fills with local products, jewelry, and handmade goods, creating a lively yet always relaxed atmosphere.

Pause for a moment near the bell tower. It is the focal point of the square and serves as a landmark for everyone who passes through. Families sit on the benches, travelers stop for a coffee, and the ocean breeze moves gently through the arches.

As you continue walking, notice how the light changes across the white façades, especially in the late afternoon. Everything feels slow, warm, and welcoming, like a small escape from the busier tourist streets of Corralejo.
