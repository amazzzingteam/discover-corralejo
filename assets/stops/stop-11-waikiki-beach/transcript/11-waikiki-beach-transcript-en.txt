Now stop and look in front of you. Can you feel the wind? Here, it is the true star of the landscape.

What you see is known as "Waikiki" by local surfers. It is not an official beach, but a surf spot that has come to life thanks to the consistent waves and the soft sand of northern Fuerteventura. It is a place where the sea always seems to be in motion, even when the ocean appears calm from afar.

Look out at the water. Can you see the surfboards waiting for the next wave? Many of the surfers are beginners, and that is one of the reasons why this place feels so lively and full of energy. Here, everyone learns, falls, gets back up, and tries again.

Now turn slightly toward the dunes behind you. The landscape changes completely: golden sand, wind shaping ever-changing patterns, and the silence of the desert meeting the ocean.

Listen carefully. The sound of the wind, the waves, and the distant voices creates a kind of continuous rhythm. It is the natural heartbeat of this corner of the island.
