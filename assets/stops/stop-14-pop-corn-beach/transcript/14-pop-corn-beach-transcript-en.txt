Hello! Welcome to one of the most curious and magical places in all of Fuerteventura: the

Popcorn Beach. You'll feel like you've just stepped into a movie, but don't be fooled:

Those "popcorns" you see scattered everywhere are not a snack! What looks like a

The expanse of tasty white corn kernels is actually made up of rhodoliths, a particular type of algae

limestones which, once detached from the ocean floor, are shaped by the force of the

waves and deposited here on the shore. It is a rare and fascinating natural phenomenon that transforms the

beach in a unique setting. It's definitely worth stopping here for

take a creative photo. I recommend you look closely at the shape of each individual

"chicco": it's amazing to see how nature can create such precise and

geometric. A small, important recommendation: please do not take away

not even a small fragment as a souvenir. These formations take decades, if

not centuries, to form; leaving them in their place, we allow all visitors who

They will come after us to enjoy this wonder. Now that you have immortalized this place
