You are on the Avenida Marítima in Puerto del Rosario. Look ahead: the Atlantic stretches out immensely, with waves that change color with the wind and clouds. To your right, you can feel the activity of the port, with ferries and boats moving slowly, almost unhurriedly. To your left begins the promenade, where you walk among sculptures that appear suddenly; some are whimsical, others more abstract, as if the city had decided to tell its story through art.

Stop for a moment and listen. Here, the wind is not just air: it is an element that shapes everything, from the sound of the sea to the rhythm of your steps. This is the everyday heart of Fuerteventura's capital, a place shared by those who work in the port, those who stroll at sunset, and those who arrive from afar.

As you continue walking, imagine the story of this coast: from a small jetty to becoming the vital center of the island. And before you, the sea remains ever-present, infinite, like a promise of constant travel
