Welcome to one of Corralejo's most colourful landmarks-the Rainbow Bench.

At first glance, it may look like a simple place to rest, but it has become a symbol of the town's warm, welcoming and international atmosphere. Set against the deep blue Atlantic, the vibrant colours create one of the most photographed spots on the promenade.

Fuerteventura has long attracted people from around the world, drawn by its beaches, relaxed lifestyle and year-round sunshine. Over the years, the island has also earned a reputation for being open, tolerant and inclusive, where people from different cultures, backgrounds and identities feel at home. The rainbow colours reflect these values of diversity, respect and community.

Take a seat for a moment and look out towards the ocean. On clear days, you can admire the turquoise waters stretching towards Lobos Island while enjoying the gentle sea breeze that makes Corralejo so special.

This is a wonderful place to pause, take a photograph and simply appreciate the relaxed rhythm of island life.
